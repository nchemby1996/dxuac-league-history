# DJUAC League History

A public, evidence-backed history of the D.J.U.A.C. fantasy football league.

The publication site will be generated only from validated historical data. The 2026 season remains in progress and will not be written into the historical publication until the season is complete.

## Data policy

- Public pages use approved displayed ESPN owner names.
- Private raw source artifacts and commissioner intelligence are excluded.
- Unresolved identity conflicts are withheld pending commissioner confirmation.
- Each published claim will carry season/category provenance and an evidence status.

Source extraction and private analysis live in separate private repositories.
