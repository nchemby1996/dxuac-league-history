# The Prophecy of "2X Champ": Jesse Christman Names His Team, Then Earns It

Jesse Christman went into 2019 with one championship (2016) and a team called *2X Champ*. By the end of December the name was accurate. The 3-seed beat Tanner Forrest, Drew Martin and Luke Martin by an average of 25.7 points and won his second title. Two things made it memorable: a late free-agent pickup in the championship game, and the luckiest season in league history playing out on the other side of the bracket.

FAAB arrived this year with a $1,000 budget, and the league treated it carefully. The biggest single bid was John Barrows's $400 on Bo Scarbrough in Week 12, which bought one start and 6 points. Jesse spent $384 on Hunter Henry in Week 7 and got 68 points over eight starts. Drew Martin spent the most ($995 of $1,000), and Tyler Harris spent $52 all year. Jesse's best move cost nothing. He signed Ryan Fitzpatrick as a free agent in Week 16 and started him in the championship game, and Fitzpatrick threw up 40 points. There were only two trades, both in Week 10. Nate Hemby got Emmanuel Sanders from Barrows for Jamaal Williams, and Tanner sent Damien Williams to Drew for Tyler Boyd.

Now the luck. Kyle Loy's *country roads take mahomes* scored the fewest points in the league (1,420.5, scoring index 86.0) and had the league's worst all-play record at 30-77. They also started 5-0, finished 7-5 and took the No. 5 seed and a bye. That's +3.62 wins above expectation, the luckiest season in the record book. Then the bracket kept the gift going. Kyle beat Josh Book 123-120 in the quarterfinals, lost a 182-165 semifinal to Luke and beat Drew 156-138 for third place. The league's lowest-scoring team finished third. Drew had the opposite year: a league-best 1,871 points and a 113.2 scoring index, eight wins and no medal.

Josh had the best draft pick in league history and still lost to Kyle. Christian McCaffrey at No. 3 overall scored 442 points, worth +291 over replacement, and the record book ranks him the most valuable pick of all time. Nate made the steal of the year in the 12th round (pick 115), where he found Lamar Jackson. Lamar scored 486 points (+180 value), and Nate started him for 436 of them. It's the second-best late-round pick in league history. It got Nate a 6-6 record, the No. 7 seed and a 160-80 first-round win over Tyler Harris. Nate had also beaten Tyler 160-159 in Week 1, so he scored exactly 160 in both games. Drew then edged Nate 143-134.5. The busts were rough. Jesse's third-round Antonio Brown (pick 30) scored 12 points for -146 of value. Nate spent No. 6 overall on David Johnson (-37). Kyle's No. 1 overall pick, Saquon Barkley, was worth a modest +76.

Luke Martin earned the top seed at 9-3 by winning his last four, then beat Ethan Hess 192-119 and Kyle 182-165 to reach his fourth title game. Jesse got there with a 147-138 win over Tanner and a 155-124 win over Drew. Michael Thomas, his first-round pick and the third most valuable player in the draft (+152), scored 25, 27 and 27 across the three playoff games. In the final, Fitzpatrick's 40 and Thomas's 27 carried Jesse past Luke 161-124. Luke is now 1-3 in championship games.

At the bottom, Tyler Harris's *The Horny Buffalos* opened 0-6 and finished 2-10. Tyler also had the season's worst luck (-2.5) and then the 160-80 playoff exit. Ethan Hess went 3-9 but beat John Barrows 171.5-110 in Round 1 before running into Luke. The season's high was Drew's 212.5 against Jesse in Week 8. The low was Kyle's 67.5 against Nate in Week 12.

## By the numbers

- **+3.62**: Kyle Loy's luck rating, the luckiest season in league history, and the lowest-scoring team in the league finished third
- **+291**: Christian McCaffrey's value over replacement, the most valuable draft pick in league history
- **115**: the pick Nate Hemby used on Lamar Jackson (486 points, +180)
- **40**: points from Ryan Fitzpatrick, a Week 16 free agent, in Jesse Christman's 161-124 title win
- **$400**: the biggest FAAB bid of the first FAAB season, which bought Bo Scarbrough and 6 points
- **1-3**: Luke Martin's record in title games after this loss
