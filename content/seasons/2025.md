# Burrow for the Ring: John Barrows Beats Tanner Forrest in Another Title Game

The league trimmed its rosters in 2025. Benches went from eight spots to five, a second flex was added, the head-coach slot was dropped and scores now ran to the hundredth of a point. The decimals showed up almost right away. In week 4, Luke Martin's Country Roads Take Mahomes beat James Brooks 144.5-144.48, a 0.02-point margin that is the narrowest win in league history. Luke Martin's only loss came in week 5, 193.08-161.52 to Drew Martin, and he won every game after that. He finished 12-1, the best regular-season record the league has ever seen, with a 111.2 scoring index and an eight-game winning streak that carries into next season. Jesse Christman went 10-3 for the 2 seed. John Barrows (9-4) took the 3 seed while leading the league with 2,169.12 points (112.9 index), and Tanner Forrest (9-4) took the last bye.

It was also the busiest trade season on record: 16 trades, with Christman and Forrest each in eight of them. Ja'Marr Chase, Forrest's No. 1 overall pick, went to Christman in week 6 for George Pickens and Kenneth Walker III, then came back in week 12 for Drake London. London was traded four times. In week 9, Kyle Loy sent Puka Nacua, his second-rounder and the second-most valuable pick of the draft (+254.2 VOR), to Forrest for Harold Fannin Jr. Barrows made one trade and it was a big one: in week 5 he gave Drew Martin Ladd McConkey and Chase Brown for Jaxon Smith-Njigba, a fourth-round pick worth +247.1.

The bottom of the standings set records. Ethan Hess's Aaron Hernandez's Taxi Service scored 1,429.1 points, a 75.2 index, the lowest in league history, with five weekly lows. His week 8 score was cut by a 15.4-point commissioner adjustment to an official 79.08. He would have lost to Christman anyway. Hess also drafted Drake Maye in round 15 and dropped him in week 2, and Forrest picked him up as a free agent. Maye turned out to be the best late-round value of the draft (+123.82). Josh Book went 1-12, tied for the worst record in league history, even though his 1,833.86 points outscored three other teams. Nobody allowed more points (2,121.1), and his -4.28 luck score is the second-worst ever. His first-round pick, Malik Nabers, returned 64.1 points and -75.7 VOR. Book lost his last seven games, a streak that also carries into next season.

The playoffs turned on a 4-9 team. Tyler Harris, the 9 seed, beat Nate Hemby 159.26-79.2 in the first round and then took on the 12-1 top seed. ESPN shows Harris starting only 10 players in an 11-man lineup, and he won anyway, 117.40-113.86, behind Brock Purdy's 31.2. It was the fifth time a No. 1 seed lost its first playoff game, and Luke Martin is now three-for-three in No. 1 seeds without a title from that spot. Barrows beat Loy 184.3-160.8, Forrest beat Tanner Edwards 167.6-148.38, and Brooks knocked out Christman 178.82-167.56. In the semifinals Forrest put up 243.7, the second-highest winners-bracket score since 2012, with Nacua scoring 52.5 against Harris. Barrows beat Brooks 165.12-150.08.

Barrows had dropped Joe Burrow before week 16. For the final he spent $525 to claim him back, and Burrow scored 26.4. Bijan Robinson, the No. 2 overall pick and the core of the draft's best class (2.22 index, second-best ever), scored 45.9. Barrows won 177.1-143.24. Maye scored 42.44 for Forrest, and it wasn't enough. Barrows now has two titles, 2020 and 2025, and both came against Forrest. He is 10-5 against Forrest all-time, 2-0 in finals. Forrest is 0-5 in championship games.

Harris, at 4-9, finished third. Book finished tenth. Hess was 11th. Hemby, the 8 seed, finished 12th.

## By the numbers

- **12-1**: Luke Martin's record, the best regular season in league history.
- **0.02**: Luke Martin's week 4 margin over James Brooks, the narrowest win ever.
- **16**: trades, the most in any season with trade data.
- **75.2**: Ethan Hess's scoring index, the lowest in league history.
- **$525**: what Barrows paid to bring back Joe Burrow for the final; Burrow scored 26.4.
- **0-5**: Tanner Forrest's record in title games.
