# One Point, Eight Seed: The Kitties Steal Josh Book's Trick

The year after Josh Book won the league as an 8 seed, an 8 seed came for Josh Book.

Joshy Woshy Puddin' N' Pie spent the regular season looking like a dynasty that had found its second wind. Book won his first four, lost only twice and finished 11-2, the fifth-best record in league history and the No. 1 seed. Look a little closer, though, and the paint was thin. His scoring index ranked sixth in the league, he never once posted the week's high score, and his 3.28 wins above his all-play record made it the third-luckiest season ever. The best team on paper was James Brooks's Twiggity Twiggity, who led the league in scoring and settled for the 3 seed.

Tanner Edwards was nowhere near any of this. Kitties and Samsquanches sat at 5-7 going into the final week. Then Edwards hung 248.5 on Kyle Loy, the highest score of the season, in a blowout that ranks among the most lopsided games in league history. He finished 6-7 and grabbed the 8 seed. Nobody was scared. They should have been.

Edwards beat Drew Martin 191.5-97 in the first round, then drew Book. What followed was the game of the season, and it was decided by a 19th-round pick. Sam LaPorta, taken 220th overall, scored 28. Book's Gabe Davis and Dalton Kincaid scored zero. Edwards even survived negative points from his Jaguars Coach. Final: 151.5-150.5. The 11-2 juggernaut was out by one point, and for the second straight year the No. 1 seed lost its first playoff game. Book knew the 8-seed trick better than anybody. He'd just never been on the receiving end.

The other side of the bracket had its own fun. Kyle Loy, the 12 seed at 2-11, knocked out Jesse Christman in round one, which is the most Ambulance thing that could happen. Nate Hemby, the 7 seed, then ran over John Barrows 190.5-105.5 and looked dangerous for about a week. Luke Martin ended that in the semifinal, 177.5-142.5. It was the third time Luke Martin had knocked Hemby out of the playoffs. Hemby has never beaten him there.

Edwards beat Tyler Harris in his semifinal behind 58 points from Amari Cooper, and the final matched two 6-7 teams: the Kitties against Luke Martin's Praise the sun god Amon-Ra. Lamar Jackson scored 50, and Edwards won 180-169.5. Luke Martin lost a title game by 10.5 points. File that away.

Edwards, in just his third season, became the ninth different owner to win the league and the second straight 8 seed to do it. After the first-round rout he won three playoff games by a combined 32.5 points. Not dominant. Just never wrong at the wrong time.

The rest of the year was quiet. The league made two trades all season, both involving Tanner Forrest, who went 4-9 for his trouble. Book's second-round pick, Nick Chubb, gave him 24 points all year. And Loy's Ambulance went 2-11 with a seven-game losing streak, which made the first-round upset even funnier.

## By the numbers

- **1**: the margin, in points, between the 8 seed and the 11-2 top seed.
- **220**: the pick Edwards spent on Sam LaPorta, who scored 28 in that game.
- **6-7**: the record of both finalists. The 11-2 team watched.
- **3.28**: Book's wins above his all-play record, the third-luckiest season ever. The luck ran out in December.
- **24**: points from Nick Chubb, Book's second-round pick, all season.
- **2**: trades, both involving Tanner Forrest, who went 4-9.
