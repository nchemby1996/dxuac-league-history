## Through Week 11 (updated Nov. 25)

The letter is J, so the target is John Barrows. The defending champion lost to Kyle Loy in Week 11 and is 4-7, the No. 10 seed. Two regular-season weeks remain, and the letter doesn't change hands until the title game.

### The top of the table

Kyle Loy's *Etienne the lamb* and James Brooks's *Twiggity Twiggity* are 8-3, the No. 1 and No. 2 seeds. Brooks has won six straight after an 0-2 start. Behind them, Ethan Hess, Luke Martin and Tanner Forrest are 7-4. Forrest was the No. 1 seed after Week 10 and is now No. 5. Seeds 1 through 4 got first-round byes in each of the last three seasons.

### The scoring

Luke Martin's *DJ's Back* leads the league with 1,801.84 points, 94.40 more than anyone else. His 246.58 in Week 10 is still the highest score anyone has posted this season. Kyle Loy's 164.12 was the best of Week 11.

### The wrong end

Nate Hemby is 4-7, the No. 9 seed, on a four-game losing streak. His 88.40 in Week 11 was the lowest score of the week. His 85.90 in Week 8 is the lowest of the season, and his 1,308.74 points are the fewest in the league. Jesse Christman is 2-9, last in the league, on a six-game losing streak. The Golden Shitter goes by postseason finish, so nothing is engraved yet.

### A record changes hands, then a turnaround

Josh Book lost the last seven regular-season games of 2025 and the first five of 2026. That 12-game regular-season losing streak is the longest in league history, one more than John Barrows's 11 in 2013-14. Josh has won five of six since, the last four in a row, and is 5-6 as the No. 8 seed.
