## Through Week 10 (updated Nov. 18)

The letter is J, so the target is John Barrows. The defending champion is 4-6 and seeded eighth. He ended a four-game losing streak last week. Three regular-season weeks remain, and the letter doesn't change hands until the title game.

### The top of the table

Tanner Forrest's *DEAN* is 7-3 and the No. 1 seed on three straight wins, with 1,508.66 points. Forrest is also 0-5 in championship games. Kyle Loy's *Etienne the lamb* is 7-3 and has never played in one. James Brooks's *Twiggity Twiggity* has won five straight and sits third at 7-3.

### The scoring

Luke Martin's *DJ's Back* leads the league with 1,655.20 points, 93.62 clear of second. His 246.58 against Tanner Edwards in Week 10 is the highest score anyone has posted this season. He owns three of the season's four biggest. He is 6-4 and the No. 5 seed.

### The wrong end

Nate Hemby is 4-6 and the No. 10 seed on a three-game losing streak. His 85.90 in Week 8 is the lowest score anyone has posted this season. Jesse Christman is 2-8 with the worst record in the league on a five-game losing streak. The Golden Shitter goes by postseason finish, so nothing is engraved yet.

### A record changes hands

Josh Book lost the last seven regular-season games of 2025 and the first five of 2026. That 12-game regular-season losing streak is the longest in league history, one more than John Barrows's 11 in 2013-14. Josh has won three straight since and is 4-6 as the No. 9 seed.
