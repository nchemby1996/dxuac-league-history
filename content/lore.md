## The letter

D.J.U.A.C. stands for **Defeat ____ Under All Circumstances League**. Every season, before a single pick is made, the blank becomes the defending champion's first initial. Win the league and the league renames itself after you. Not as an honor. As a target.

It has mostly been a J. Josh Book, Jesse Christman and John Barrows have combined for 11 of the 19 titles, so the rest of the league has spent most of two decades trying to Defeat J. Everybody's trying to take the letter.

## The Sword

The champion doesn't get a trophy. He gets the Sword. It's passed from winner to winner at the start of every season, before the draft, and its first job every year is ceremonial: cutting the watermelon that opens the season. Josh Book has been handed it seven times. Everyone who has held it is on the [home page](../index.html), next to the letter they put on the league.

## The Golden Shitter

There is always a last-place punishment. Once it's served, the Golden Shitter changes hands: a toilet seat engraved with the name of every loser in league history. The Sword goes to the top of the league. The Shitter goes to the bottom. Both get passed on. Nobody asks for either one back.

The Ledger has finished transcribing the engravings. Last place means last in the regular season, one name per year, 2007 through 2025.
