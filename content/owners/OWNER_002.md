# No Hope, Country Roads: The Unluckiest Man in D.J.U.A.C.

Every league needs someone the schedule hates. Ours has Kyle Loy, and the math backs him up. Over 14 seasons he has won 9.41 fewer games than his all-play record says he should have, the worst luck number of any owner in league history. He is 66-107-1, has never reached a title game, and has spent most of his career finding new and creative ways to have it be someone else's fault.

He was there at the founding, and the founding was cruel. Karl Loy opened 2007 at 0-7 and finished 3-11, dead last, despite ranking fourth of eight in points. His -3.72 luck rating is still among the worst seasons ever recorded. He did hang 947 on Nate Hemby in Week 13, a small act of revenge against the eventual top seed. In 2008 he spent the No. 3 overall pick on Tom Brady and got 2 points, one of the five biggest early-round busts in league history. He has drafted Brady five times, which is commitment.

Then he left for five seasons (2009-2013) and came back to a league that had apparently been saving up luck for him. Team ShortRound opened 2014 at 4-0 and went 7-5 with a bye on an 88.5 scoring index. The next two years, under a team name this site will print exactly once (Suck My Dick), he reached back-to-back semifinals, including a 199-114.5 demolition of Drew Martin in the 2016 quarterfinals.

The defining stretch came in back-to-back seasons that should be studied in statistics classes. In 2019, country roads take mahomes scored the fewest points in the league (1,420.5), went 30-77 in all-play, started 5-0 anyway, finished 7-5 with a bye, beat Josh Book 123-120 in the quarterfinals and took third place. That's +3.62 wins above expectation, the luckiest season in league history. The universe sent the bill the very next year. No hope Pats scored 1,785.5, third-most in the league, and put up 255 against John Barrows in Week 7, the best era-adjusted single-week score the league has ever seen. They went 2-10. The -4.34 luck rating is the worst season on record. Kyle holds both ends of the luck book, one year apart.

The Ambulance era has been rough sledding: 2-11 in 2023, then 3-10 last season. But Kyle keeps showing up in December. The 2023 Ambulance was the 12 seed and still knocked out Jesse Christman 136.5-128 in round one. In 2024 the 11 seed hung 247 on Tanner Edwards in the first round, behind 65 from Josh Allen, the highest winners-bracket score since 2012. Last season he traded Puka Nacua, his second-round pick and a +254.2 value monster, to Tanner Forrest for Harold Fannin Jr. Nacua then scored 52.5 for Forrest in a semifinal. Kyle had already been eliminated, which is about the only way that trade could have gotten worse.

The rivalries mostly involve getting beaten. Book leads him 16-8 in the regular season, Nate leads 13-5. The exception is Drew Martin, whom Kyle has beaten in three of their five playoff meetings.

## Career at a glance

- **66-107-1** in the regular season over 14 seasons
- **0** championship games, **3** semifinals, **4** first-round byes
- **-9.41** career luck, the unluckiest in league history
- **2019**: luckiest season ever (+3.62), and his best finish, third
- **2020**: unluckiest season ever (-4.34), plus the best era-adjusted week in league history (255)
- **24** weekly low scores, third-most all time
