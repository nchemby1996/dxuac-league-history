# I Don't Knows: The Punter Mogul

Ryan Hemby played exactly one season, and he made the most of the weirdest scoring system the league ever had. His I Don't Knows held the No. 1 overall pick in the first D.J.U.A.C. draft and took LaDainian Tomlinson, who returned 982 points. Then, in the 15th round, he took punter Dustin Colquitt. Colquitt scored 2,689 points, +614.5 against replacement, the second-most valuable pick of the entire 2007 draft. Ryan's class ranked first of eight. In 2007, that's what a punter could do for you.

The record was harder work. He sat at 3-7 after 10 weeks, then won his last four, including a 714.5-714 escape against Kyle Loy in Week 11, to finish 7-7 as the No. 4 seed. In the first round he knocked out Curtiss Hemby, 668.5-628.5. Then Nate Hemby hung 942.5 on him in the semifinals, winning by 248.5. Ryan lost the third-place game to Dylan Plouvier, 798-674.5, and finished fourth.

One season, one playoff win, one historically valuable punter. Not a bad résumé for a team called I Don't Knows.
