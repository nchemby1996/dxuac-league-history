# Team panky: The League's First Great Team Without a Ring

Dylan Plouvier played two seasons. The first was excellent. Team panky won six straight from Week 2 to Week 7, stood 6-1, and finished 10-4 with 10,596 points, a 114.5 scoring index and the No. 2 seed. He was also the victim of the league's most famous early number: in Week 14 Nate Hemby hung 981 on him, still the highest raw score in league history. In Week 9 he scored 842 against Nate and lost again, the second-highest-scoring loss in league history after adjusting for era.

The bye didn't help. Josh Book beat him 670-612 in the semifinals on the way to the first title, and Plouvier settled for third with a 798-674.5 win over Ryan Hemby.

His draft had the best and worst of 2007. Tom Brady at No. 5 scored 1,462 points. Maurice Jones-Drew in round four (+516) and Kellen Winslow in round 10 (+469) were steals. But Marvin Harrison in round two scored 216 points, -552 VOR, the worst early pick of the draft.

Year two was a different story. Under the team name ". PLOUV" (yes, with the period), he went 3-8 and lost his first-round game to Kyle Loy, 178-160.

## Career at a glance

- **13-12** in the regular season over two seasons
- **1** first-round bye, **0-2** in winners-bracket games
- **10-4**: the 2007 record that earned the No. 2 seed
- **-552**: Marvin Harrison's 2007 value, the worst early pick of the league's first draft
