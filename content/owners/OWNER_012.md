# GIGGITY PWNERS: Five Playoff Trips, Zero Playoff Wins

Brandon Mlekus played five seasons, made the playoff bracket in all five of them, and lost his first game every single time. He's 0-5 in winners-bracket games, which takes a special kind of consistency. In between, he drafted like a genius and caught more breaks than almost anyone in league history.

His debut, under the inspired team name no name, went 4-7. As the No. 8 seed he lost to No. 9 Curtiss Hemby 173.5-159.5 and finished 11th of 11. As GIGGITY PWNERS in 2009 he scored 213 on Josh Book in Week 8, the 10th-best era-adjusted week in league history, took the No. 3 seed and lost to No. 6 John Barrows 188.5-137.

Then the draft table started cooking. In 2010 HAWANTMADADS STUDS took Arian Foster with the 133rd pick, in round 14. Foster scored 294 points, +165 against replacement, still the best late-round pick in league history. They lost to Seth Chandler 118-116 in round one, and Chandler went on to win the title. In 2011 THE BEAST MODE IS ON drafted the best class in league history (a 2.48 index), led by Tom Brady at No. 3 (+135). Mlekus went 7-5, took the No. 2 seed and beat Nate Hemby 130-122.5 in Week 12, a week after Nate's 15-game winning streak ended. Book beat him 312.5-259.5 in the two-week semifinal. Mlekus did win the third-place game, 254-226 over Chandler, which is the closest he ever came to a playoff win that counted.

His farewell season was a luck masterpiece. Vilma, The Bounty Hunter scored the fewest points in the league (1,115.5), drafted the worst class, and still went 6-6 with a bye. That's +3.55 wins above expectation, the second-luckiest season on record, and he was carrying commissioner penalties at the time. Two of them flipped results: a 93.5-85.5 win over Chandler became a 73.5-85.5 loss, and a 110-103 win over Nate became a 90-103 loss. Luke Martin ended it in the quarterfinals, 136-91.5.

## Career at a glance

- **30-31** in the regular season over five seasons (2008-2012)
- **5** playoff appearances, **0-5** in winners-bracket games
- **+5.43** career luck, third-luckiest in league history
- **2.48**: his 2011 draft class index, the best ever
- **No. 133**: Arian Foster in 2010, the best late-round pick ever
