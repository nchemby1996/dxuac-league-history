# Team Hemby: One Season, One Final, One Bad Night

Caleb Hemby played exactly one season, 2013, and got further than a lot of lifers ever have.

*Team Hemby* went 7-5 and took the No. 5 seed, which that year came with a bye. It wasn't a scary team on paper. Its 96.4 scoring index was below the league average. Then the bracket opened up. Caleb outlasted Drew Martin 175-157.5 in the quarterfinals and beat top seed Tanner Forrest 105-97 in the semifinals, his second win over Forrest that season. A one-and-done owner was in the title game.

The final was a different story. Luke Martin beat him 179.5-101, the second-largest margin in championship-game history. Caleb went home with a runner-up finish in his only year, which still counts.

He had other moments. His Week 9 win over Tyler Harris, 189.5-89.5, is among the most lopsided in league history once adjusted for era. His 11th-round Chiefs D/ST returned +114 VOR, one of the better late-round picks on record. His first-round pick, Trent Richardson at No. 8, scored 114 points, which did not help.

## Career at a glance

- **7-5** in his only season (2013), No. 5 seed with a bye
- **Runner-up** after two playoff wins
- **2-0** against top seed Tanner Forrest
- **179.5-101:** the title-game loss to Luke Martin
- **+114:** VOR on his 11th-round Chiefs D/ST
