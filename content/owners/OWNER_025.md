# Twiggity Twiggity: The Best Win Percentage in League History, Still Waiting on the Ring

James Brooks walked into the league's first 12-team season in 2021 and beat Nate Hemby 181-180.5 in his first game. Then he led the league with 2,099.5 points without spending a single FAAB dollar. Subtle, he is not.

Five seasons in, he has never had a losing record. His .600 win percentage and 105.44 career scoring index are both the best in league history among owners with five or more seasons, and he has earned a bye in four of his five years. After two seasons of team names this site will not be reprinting, he settled on *Twiggity Twiggity* and has kept it for three.

December has been a different sport. In 2021 he routed Nate 155.5-81, then Tanner Forrest dropped 206.5 on him in the semifinals. In 2022 his draft class ranked 12th of 12 and he reached the final anyway, surviving a 154-154 quarterfinal tie with Nate as the higher seed and beating Jesse Christman. Josh Book won the title game 111.5-93.5, and Justin Jefferson, Brooks's No. 2 overall pick, scored 3 points in it. In 2023 he led the league in scoring again with 2,107 points and lost his first playoff game to Luke Martin, 136.5-134. In 2024 Luke ended him again, 151.5-105. In 2025 his class ranked last again even though Christian McCaffrey, his No. 8 pick, returned +285.4 VOR. He beat Ethan Hess 169.84-77.76 and Christman, then lost his semifinal to John Barrows. That season also handed him the narrowest loss in league history, 144.5-144.48 to Luke.

Barrows is the problem. Brooks is 3-8 against him, his worst record against anyone. Ethan Hess is the opposite: Brooks is 6-0 against him, and 6-1 against Nate, including both of their playoff meetings.

He is also a willing dealer, with nine trades since arriving, four of them with Drew Martin. In 2024 he sent Bijan Robinson and Courtland Sutton to Christman for Saquon Barkley.

## Career at a glance

- **39-26** in the regular season, a .600 win percentage, the best in league history
- **0 titles**, 1 final (2022)
- **4 byes** in five seasons
- **2 league scoring titles** (2021, 2023)
- **105.44:** his career scoring index, the best in league history
- **0-2** against Luke Martin in the playoffs
