# Little Boy Pink: Third in Points, Last One Out

Noah Monsour played one season, 2009, and spent it proving that points and wins are only loosely related.

*little boy pink* scored 2,018 points, third-most in the league. It went 5-8. Six of the eight teams made the playoffs. The two that didn't were Jesse Christman's 1-12 team and Monsour's. His luck rating of -2.28 wins was the worst in the league.

There was a stretch in the middle when it looked like it might work. He won three straight from Weeks 5 through 7, including a 209-114 demolition of Jesse Christman and a 221-point afternoon against Brandon Mlekus. Then he lost his next five. Nate Hemby beat him all three times they played.

The draft was respectable, with Larry Fitzgerald at No. 2, Tom Brady in the second round and Frank Gore in the fifth, the best value of his class. It just never turned into a playoff ticket.

He did end the year in a familiar spot: the consolation bracket, playing Christman. He lost the first meeting 142-133, then won the next two. One season, and Monsour still went 3-1 against the guy who would later win two titles.

## Career at a glance

- **5-8** in his only season (2009)
- **2,018** points, third in the league, and no playoff spot
- **-2.28:** luck rating, the worst in the league that year
- **221:** his season high, against Brandon Mlekus
- **3-1** against Jesse Christman
