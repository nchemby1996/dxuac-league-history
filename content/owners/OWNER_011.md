# Team Saltines: The Best One-and-Done in League History

Tyler Nystrom played one season. It was very good. Team Saltines opened 2008 at 5-0 and finished 9-2, an .818 winning percentage that's the ninth-best regular season in league history and by far the best of any one-season owner. He lost the No. 1 seed only because Tyler Harris went 9-1-1.

He did it at the draft table. His 2008 class index of 2.02 led the league and ranks sixth-best in league history. The heavy lifting came late: Michael Turner in round eight (394 points, +159 VOR) and Andre Johnson in round nine (413 points, +150). Larry Fitzgerald in round two added 419 points.

The No. 2 seed got a bye and rolled through two rounds, beating Kyle Loy 188-157.5 and Zak Hensley 196.5-108. Then came Harris in the final. Nystrom scored 174. Harris scored 196.5. Saltines finished second and never played another season.

## Career at a glance

- **9-2** in his only season (2008), the best record of any one-season owner
- **1** championship game, lost to Tyler Harris 196.5-174
- **2.02**: his draft class index, best in the league that year
- **0** seasons played after the title game
