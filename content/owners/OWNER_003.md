# The Pittsburg Gorillas: Fast Start, Long Fall

Curtiss Hemby lasted two seasons in D.J.U.A.C., and both of them were the Pittsburg Gorillas. For about three weeks in 2007, they were the best team in the league. The Gorillas opened 3-0. Then they lost four straight. In Week 5 Ryan Hemby held them to 251.5, the eighth-lowest era-adjusted score in league history. A week later John Barrows beat them 899-495, a 404-point margin. Curtiss finished 6-8, snuck in as the No. 5 seed and lost his first playoff game to Ryan, 668.5-628.5.

Year two opened 0-3, completing a seven-game losing streak that spanned both seasons. But the Gorillas had one great moment: a 162.5-162.5 tie with Tyler Harris in Week 8, one of only two games the eventual champion failed to win all year. At 3-7-1 and the No. 9 seed, Curtiss then upset No. 8 Brandon Mlekus 173.5-159.5 before Harris ended it, 188.5-153.

The draft file explains the arc. Rudi Johnson at No. 4 overall in 2007 went -258 against replacement. But T.J. Houshmandzadeh in round seven (+451) and Philip Rivers in round nine of 2008 (+142) were real finds, and his 2008 class ranked second of 11. Good drafting, bad Sundays.
