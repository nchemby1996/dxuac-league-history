# Kitties and Samsquanches: The Champion Who Never Needed a Bye

Tanner Edwards joined in 2021, named his team *Kitties and Samsquanches* and has never changed it. He has also never earned a first-round bye. He has a ring anyway.

The first two years were solid and short. He went 8-5 in both, beat Jesse Christman 157-143.5 in his first playoff game and Tanner Forrest 141.5-95.5 in his second year, and lost the next round both times. The 2022 team won seven straight in the regular season. His best early find came in the 2021 draft: Deebo Samuel in the 11th round, No. 122 overall, worth +162 VOR, the sixth-best late-round pick in league history.

Then came 2023. Edwards lost four of five in the middle of the season and sat at 5-7 heading into the final week. He put up 248.5 on Kyle Loy, the season's highest score, finished 6-7 and slid in as the No. 8 seed. Then he went on a run nobody in the league will let him forget: 191.5-97 over Drew Martin, 151.5-150.5 over the 11-2 top seed Josh Book, 149-128 over Tyler Harris, and 180-169.5 over Luke Martin in a final between two 6-7 teams. Sam LaPorta, his 19th-round pick and the 220th player taken, scored 28 in the one-point win. Edwards became the ninth different owner to win the league. His only losing season is the one with the trophy.

He hasn't been back to a semifinal since. Loy got his revenge in the 2024 first round, 247-156.5. That year's No. 10 overall pick, Isiah Pacheco, scored 52 points. In 2025 he beat Book 135.78-128.64 in the first round and lost to Forrest.

The record book has a word for this career: lucky. Edwards has won 6.11 more games than his all-play record says he should have, second only to Book. He's 2-1 against Book in the playoffs, too. The one owner he cannot solve is Ethan Hess, who is 6-0 against him.

## Career at a glance

- **37-28** in the regular season over five seasons
- **1 title (2023)**, won from the No. 8 seed with four playoff wins
- **0 byes**
- **7-4** in winners-bracket games
- **+6.11:** wins above all-play expectation, second-luckiest career in league history
- **248.5:** his career-high score, in the week that saved the 2023 season
