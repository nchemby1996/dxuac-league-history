# Team Mini Denny: Six and Oh, Then Oh No

Dennis Hensley played three seasons, 2010 through 2012, and packed a full career of whiplash into them.

He arrived in the 2010 expansion class and promptly took over the league. *Team Mini Denny* opened 6-0, stood 10-1 after Week 11 and locked up the No. 1 seed at 10-3. It was not quite as good as it looked. Hensley ranked fourth in points, and his +2.72 luck rating was the best in the league. He lost his last two regular-season games, spent the bye watching, lost his semifinal to Jesse Christman 121-94.5 and lost the third-place game too. The 10-1 team finished fourth, on four straight losses.

He also started his career by drafting Chris Johnson first overall, one season after Johnson's 2,000-yard year. Johnson gave him 203 points, which is fine, and ranked 15th in value, which is less fine.

The 2011 draft was the opposite story. Hensley took Aaron Rodgers at No. 8 and got 432 points, the most valuable pick of that draft, then found Cam Newton in the 10th round. He went 5-7 anyway. In Week 8 Seth Chandler beat him 159.5-47, one of the biggest beatings in league history once adjusted for era.

His exit in 2012 was all-time strange. In Week 7 he scored 32.5 against Brandon Mlekus, the third-lowest era-adjusted score in league history. He still made the bracket as the No. 8 seed, then put up 34.5 against John Barrows, the lowest score ever recorded in a winners-bracket game. And then, of course, he won all three of his consolation games. The last was a 117.5-114 win over Christman, whose 154 points had been cut by a 40-point commissioner penalty.

Seth was his nemesis, 4-2 in their six meetings. Brandon Mlekus was his mark: Hensley went 4-2 against him.

## Career at a glance

- **20-17** in the regular season over three seasons
- **1 bye**, as the 2010 No. 1 seed
- **0-2** in winners-bracket games
- **Best season:** 2010, 10-3 with a 6-0 start
- **34.5:** his 2012 first-round score, the lowest in winners-bracket history
- **+3.69:** career luck, fifth-luckiest in league history
