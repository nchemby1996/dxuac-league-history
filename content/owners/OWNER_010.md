# Zak Hensley, Bracket Buster

Zak Hensley played one season, named his team after himself, and gave the 2008 playoffs their only real shock. He went 5-6 in the regular season. One of those losses came by half a point, 164.5-164 to Nate Hemby in Week 6, the second-narrowest result in league history. He also scored 251.5 on Josh Book in Week 10.

Every team made the 2008 bracket, and Hensley used it. As the No. 6 seed he beat Tanner Forrest 183.5-109. Then he upset No. 3 Nate Hemby, who had opened 6-0, by a score of 173-153.5. Tyler Nystrom finally stopped him in the semifinals, 196.5-108. Hensley still beat Book 180.5-168.5 for third place.

His best pick was Jay Cutler in round eight (+152 VOR), the ninth-best late-round pick in league history. His worst was Darren McFadden in round two (-91). Then he was gone, a one-season owner with a better playoff record than plenty of lifers.

## Career at a glance

- **5-6** in his only season (2008)
- **2-1** in winners-bracket games, a semifinal and a third-place finish
- **173-153.5**: his upset of the No. 3 seed, Nate Hemby
- **0.5**: his margin of defeat against the same Nate Hemby in Week 6
