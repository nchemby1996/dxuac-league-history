# The Chubb Hub: Nine Seasons, One Bye and a Long Look at the Basement

Ethan Hess joined in 2017 and has been in the playoffs every year since, which in this league is like being proud of having a pulse. The real numbers are harder. He is 43-70 in the regular season, a .381 win percentage that is the worst in league history among owners with five or more seasons. He has one bye, one semifinal and no title-game appearances in nine years.

He did arrive with some nerve. His debut team posted the league's lowest scoring index, 87.5, went 5-7 and then blasted Luke Martin 139-49.5 in the first round of the playoffs before Jesse Christman ended it. In 2018 he scored 1,862.5 points, third-most in the league, with a league-high four weekly high scores, and still went 5-7 with the worst luck in the league. One of those losses was a 148.5-148 decision to Drew Martin. That's the Ethan experience: close enough to smell it.

Then came the Chubb years. *Hot Chubb Time Machine* went 5-7 in 2020. *ChubbHub CH* drafted the league's best class in 2021, with Ja'Marr Chase in round five and Joe Burrow in round 13, and beat Luke 197.5-154.5 in the first round. And in 2022 everything clicked, briefly. Ethan took Patrick Mahomes with the 20th pick, the most valuable pick of the draft at +214 VOR, built a class that rates third-best in league history and opened 7-0. With the end of 2021, that made nine straight regular-season wins, the fifth-longest streak on record. He went 1-5 the rest of the way, still took the No. 4 seed and the only bye of his career, beat Tanner Edwards 173.5-151.5 and then lost his semifinal to Josh Book 157.5-114.5.

What followed was the basement. He went 5-8, then 3-10, then 3-10. In 2024 he scored a league-low 1,588 points, lost his last seven games and took a 233-92.5 beating from Tyler Harris. In 2025 he set a record nobody wants: a 75.2 scoring index, the lowest in league history. He also drafted Drake Maye in the 15th round that year, dropped him in Week 2 and watched Tanner Forrest pick him up. Maye became the best late-round value of the draft and scored 42.44 for Forrest in the championship game.

There is one player Ethan always gets right. He has drafted Derrick Henry three times, in 2019, 2024 and 2025, and Henry returned at least +144 VOR every time.

The head-to-head ledger is rough. Ethan is 1-11 against Nate Hemby, including a playoff loss, and 0-6 against James Brooks. He is 6-13 against Drew Martin, his most frequent opponent, and 2-9 against Book. The strange part is who he does beat. Tanner Edwards, an actual champion, is 0-6 against him, playoff game included, and Tyler Harris trails him 7-4.

He also has 22 weekly low scores, fifth-most in league history, against 16 weekly highs. When the Chubb Hub hits, it hits hard. It just doesn't hit in December.

## Career at a glance

- **43-70** in the regular season over nine seasons, the worst win percentage in league history (min. five seasons)
- **0 titles, 0 finals**, 1 bye and 1 semifinal (2022)
- **5-9** in winners-bracket games
- **Best season:** 2022, a 7-0 start, the No. 4 seed and a top-three all-time draft class
- **75.2:** his 2025 scoring index, the lowest in league history
- **1-11** against Nate Hemby
