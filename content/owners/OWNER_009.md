# DEAN of the Runners-Up

Tanner Forrest has played in five championship games. He has lost five championship games. No owner in D.J.U.A.C. history has been to more finals without a ring, and only Josh Book and Luke Martin have been to more finals, period. He's the league's most reliable December guest and its least reliable closer.

It didn't start like a contender's career. Team Awesomeness went 1-9 in 2008, posted a 77.3 scoring index (the second-lowest in league history) and drafted the worst class in the league. It lost its playoff opener 183.5-109 to Zak Hensley, then scored 188 in each of its two consolation games, which is the most Team Awesomeness thing imaginable. Then Tanner left for four seasons.

He came back in 2013 a different owner. It Ertz When Eifert went 9-3 and took the No. 1 seed behind Peyton Manning, the most valuable pick of the year (+228). He beat Tyler Harris 176.5-70 in Week 10. Then Caleb Hemby beat him in Week 12, and beat him again in the semifinals, 105-97. It was a preview of the next decade: great team, wrong ending. In 2015 he managed 50 points against Drew Martin in Week 3, one of the five lowest era-adjusted scores ever.

Then came the finals, in order:

- **2016:** Dean's Nutz, the No. 1 seed, built on David Johnson at No. 8 overall (+226, the third-most valuable pick in league history). Jesse Christman beat him 157-134.5.
- **2018:** Goldens Taint, the No. 2 seed. Nate Hemby beat him 177-139.
- **2020:** Smokin' That Good Herb-ert, 9-3. He drafted Justin Jefferson in the 16th round, dropped him in Week 2 and watched John Barrows claim him in Week 4. Barrows beat him 161-140.5.
- **2021:** Gettin' Higgy With It started 7-0, extending a 10-game regular-season winning streak, the fourth-longest in league history. He scored 206.5 in the semifinal. Book beat him 152-133.5.
- **2025:** DEAN went 9-4, traded for Puka Nacua in Week 9, and got 52.5 from Nacua in a 243.7-point semifinal. Drake Maye, a Week 2 free-agent pickup, scored 42.44 in the final. Barrows beat him again, 177.1-143.24.

In between, the crashes were spectacular. A year after the 2021 final, Gettin' Higgy With It went 2-11 and finished 12th of 12. Downs Bad finished 12th again in 2023. In 2024 DEAN scored 2,052.5 points, second-most in the league, went 5-8 and spent $745 on Isaac Guerendo in Week 14.

He's also the league's second-busiest dealer, with 17 trades since 2018, six of them with Christman, the most of any pair. Christman has also beaten him all three times they've met in the playoffs. Barrows leads him 10-5 overall and 2-0 in finals. Luke Martin leads him 14-8 in the regular season. The one man he owns is Harris: Tanner is 4-0 against him in the playoffs.

## Career at a glance

- **84-87** in the regular season over 14 seasons
- **0 titles** in **5** championship games (2016, 2018, 2020, 2021, 2025)
- **9** first-round byes, third-most in league history
- **17** trades since 2018, second-most in the league
- **10** straight regular-season wins (2020-2021)
- **77.3**: his 2008 scoring index, second-lowest ever
