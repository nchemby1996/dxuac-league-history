# The Drifter: Last in the Standings, First in the Toilet Bowl

Luten Warrick played one season, 2010, under the name *THE DRIFTER 24*, and the drifting mostly went one way.

He finished the regular season 3-10, last in the league, with an 87.8 scoring index and a five-game losing streak from Weeks 8 through 12. One of his three wins was a 124.5-87 upset of Nate Hemby, who led the league in points that season. Warrick also lost to Jesse Christman by three, 121.5-118.5.

Then the season ended the way nobody expected. He won both of his consolation games, 113.5-112.5 over Dylan Flora and 111-79 over Ryan Long, and climbed to eighth place. Last place in the standings, two straight wins when it no longer mattered.

His draft had one gem. With the 132nd pick, in the 14th round, he took the Patriots Coach, who returned 313 points and +105.5 VOR, still one of the best late-round picks in league history. The early rounds did not go as well: DeAngelo Williams in the third round scored 30 points.

## Career at a glance

- **3-10** in his only season (2010), last in the regular season
- **2-0** in consolation games, finishing eighth
- **+105.5:** VOR on his 14th-round Patriots Coach
- **124.5-87:** his win over the league's points leader, Nate Hemby
