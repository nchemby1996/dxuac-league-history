# Rock Chalk Jayhawk: The First Owner of Team 30

Ryan Long played one season, 2010, as *Rock Chalk Jayhawk*. His ESPN team ID, 30, went on to belong to Luke Martin from 2012 on, and won two titles. Long's run was quieter.

He went 5-8 and finished ninth, with an 86.1 scoring index, the lowest in the league that year. He had four weekly low scores, including 45 points against Tyler Harris in Week 8. The draft had a real hit: Tom Brady with the 16th pick, worth +111 VOR.

He did get his moments. In Week 6 he beat Nate Hemby 112-95.5, and Nate led the league in points that year. In Week 9 he crushed Dylan Flora 129.5-47.5, one of the more lopsided games of the era. And in Week 12 he beat 10-1 Dennis Hensley 118.5-56.5, the first of four straight losses that ended the top seed's season.

The postseason was less kind. He lost both consolation games, to Flora and Luten Warrick.

## Career at a glance

- **5-8** in his only season (2010), ninth place
- **86.1:** his scoring index, lowest in the league that year
- **+111:** VOR on second-round Tom Brady
- **118.5-56.5:** his Week 12 win over the No. 1 seed
