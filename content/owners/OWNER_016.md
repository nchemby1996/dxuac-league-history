# Dylan Flora, by Dylan Flora

Dylan Flora joined in the 2010 expansion class and named his team *Dylan Flora*. Nobody has ever accused him of burying the lede.

The team went 5-8 and finished 10th of 10. The draft set the tone: Flora took Tony Romo with the 11th pick, in the second round, and got 122 points, -136 VOR. The record book still lists it as the third-biggest early-round bust in league history. In Week 4 he scored 36 points in a 102-36 loss to Brandon Mlekus, the season low and one of the five lowest era-adjusted scores the league has recorded.

He lived on the margins in both directions. He lost to Tyler Harris 113-112.5, the closest game of the regular season, and lost a consolation game to Luten Warrick 113.5-112.5. In between, in Week 8, he beat Seth Chandler 92.5-91.5. Seth won the championship that year. That was one of only five regular-season losses the champion took all year.

## Career at a glance

- **5-8** in his only season (2010), 10th of 10
- **36:** his Week 4 score, the season low
- **-136:** VOR on Tony Romo, the third-biggest early-round bust in league history
- **1:** his margin over eventual champion Seth Chandler
- **1.5:** the combined margin of his two closest losses
