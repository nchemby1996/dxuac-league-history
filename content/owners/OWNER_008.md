# A Win Would Be Nice: The Long, Long Wait of T Doggie

Tyler Harris has played every season this league has ever had, and he got the good part out of the way early. He won the 2008 title in the league's second year. He has spent the 17 seasons since chasing the second one, the longest active title drought in D.J.U.A.C. At some point he started naming his teams accordingly. A Win Would Be Nice, in 2014. Another Losing Season, in 2020. A man who knows his audience.

The peak was real. T Doggie went 9-1-1 in 2008, still the fourth-best regular-season record in league history. His only loss came against Josh Book in Week 2, his only other blemish was a tie with Curtiss Hemby, and in Week 12 he dropped 275.5 on Nate Hemby. He also had the year's most valuable pick, Drew Brees in round two (+224). As the No. 1 seed he beat Book 208-155 and Tyler Nystrom 196.5-174 for the title.

Then came 2009, the year that still stings. tdogg beast opened by hanging 255.5 on Jesse Christman, led the league with 2,226.5 points, went 10-3 and beat Nate both times they played. He scored 210.5 in the semifinal. Then he met Nate in the final and lost 148.5-130.5. A year later, as the No. 7 seed, he got a measure of revenge, knocking the No. 2 seed Nate out of the first round 127.5-115, before Seth Chandler beat him 107.5-99 in the semifinals.

And then, for a very long time, not much. In 2013 Give Jamaal The Ball went 3-9 while allowing the most points (by index) of any team in league history. In 2014 he drafted the most valuable pick of the year, DeMarco Murray in round three (+205), and still posted the worst draft class in league history (-2.61), because his No. 3 overall pick, Adrian Peterson, scored 9 points. A year later he spent a third-rounder on Peyton Manning and got -194 of value. In 2019 The Horny Buffalos went 2-10. Along the way he collected 33 weekly low scores, the most in league history, and won 8.5 fewer games than his all-play record predicted, the second-worst career luck in the book.

*The McAfrican Americans* got close first. They went out to Book 157-120 as the 9 seed in 2022, then took the No. 4 seed at 8-5 in 2023, beat Kyle Loy 179-125 and lost the semifinal to Tanner Edwards, 149-128.

In 2024 the drought nearly broke. April Showers Bring Zay Flowers had the league's best draft class, built on Ja'Marr Chase at No. 5 (+260), led the league with 2,158 points, beat Ethan Hess 233-92.5 and took the No. 1 seed at 9-4. He got past Nate 161-143 and Christman 123-118.5. Then Luke Martin, the No. 7 seed, beat him 186.5-167 in the final.

Last season was pure chaos. Are You Worthy? went 4-9 and slid in as the 9 seed, then hammered Nate 159.26-79.2 and knocked out 12-1 Luke Martin 117.40-113.86, despite ESPN showing only 10 starters in his lineup that week. Brock Purdy scored 31.2. He finished third and carries a five-game losing streak into next season.

The Nate rivalry is the league's most-played, at 35 games: 14-14 in the regular season, with Harris up 4-3 in the playoffs. Book leads him 18-12 in the regular season. Tanner Forrest has beaten him all four times they've met in the playoffs, and Harris has beaten Kyle Loy all three times. He has made zero trades since 2018. Whatever the plan is, he's sticking to it.

## Career at a glance

- **103-132-1** in the regular season over 19 seasons
- **1 title** (2008) in **3** championship games
- **5** first-round byes
- **17** seasons and counting since his last title, the longest active drought
- **33** weekly low scores, the most in league history
- **9-1-1**: the 2008 regular season, fourth-best ever
