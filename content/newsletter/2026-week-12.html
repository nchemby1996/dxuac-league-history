Five teams, four chairs

Seeds 1 through 4 got first-round byes in each of the last three seasons. Five teams are fighting for them with two weeks left.

Kyle Loy's Etienne the lamb and Brooks are 8-3. Kyle took the No. 1 seed by beating the target. Behind them, Ethan Hess, Luke Martin and Forrest are all 7-4, sorted for now by ESPN's tiebreakers. Ethan plays Luke this week. Luke plays Forrest the week after. Somebody is going to be mad about it.

The hot hands: Brooks has won six straight since an 0-2 start. Josh Book has won four straight, five of six, after losing his first five. The Dynasty is 5-6 and the No. 8 seed, which in this league counts as a comeback.

The cold ones: Nate Hemby has lost four straight and still climbed a seed, to No. 9, because John Barrows lost too. Jesse Christman has lost six straight and is 2-9. The Golden Shitter goes by postseason finish, so nothing is engraved yet. Jesse has two engravings already.

A first-round pick on the waiver wire

{{g:clerk_alarmed|Puka Nacua: a first-round pick, 180.6 points, unclaimed.}}

On Saturday of Week 11, James Brooks dropped Puka Nacua for Roman Wilson. Nacua was Brooks's first-round pick and has 180.6 points this season. ESPN lists him on injured reserve. As of Wednesday, nobody has claimed him. Brooks won that week anyway, his sixth straight.

A.J. Brown had a busier week. Nate Hemby, who drafted him in the second round, dropped him. Drew Martin claimed him for $50. Then Drew dropped him too. Brown has 22.8 points this season and is a free agent again.

Then a trade. Josh Book and Nate agreed to send Jared Goff to the commissioner for Jordyn Tyson. Goff scored 27.36 on Josh's bench in Week 11, and Josh won anyway. ESPN showed the deal accepted but not yet processed on Wednesday. The Ledger will report the rest when the record does.

Quick hits

Kyle Loy bought a quarterback. Last week The Ledger noted Drake Maye scored 3.02 and Kyle had no other. He bid $250 on Cam Ward, who started and scored 28.22. Etienne the lamb put up 164.12, the high of the week, and beat John Barrows by 34.12.

Tanner Edwards picked up the Panthers D/ST, started them, and got 15. Kitties and Samsquanches beat Drew Martin 145.86-132.06. The margin was 13.80. The defense outscored it. Edwards is 6-5 and the No. 6 seed.

Drew Martin, the Flounder King, made nine adds in Week 11, the most in the league. The Fumbling Flounders are 3-8. In this week's waivers he placed the top bid, $90 for Chris Rodriguez Jr.

Nate Hemby scored 88.40, the low of the week, and lost to Ethan Hess by 34.48. He came in leading that series 11-1. It's the fourth-lowest score anyone has posted this season. The lowest is also his, 85.90 in Week 8. He has 1,308.74 points, the fewest in the league. The Professor is grading on a curve.

Luke Martin beat Tyler Harris 146.64-118.80. DJ's Back leads the league with 1,801.84 points, 94.40 clear of anyone else. Tyler is 5-6 and the No. 7 seed. Josh Book beat Jesse Christman 131.36-115.76.
